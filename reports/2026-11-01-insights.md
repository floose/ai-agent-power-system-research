### Forecasts need an operating safety layer

**Paper:** [Safe machine learning–based active network control for renewable dominated distribution systems: A UK distribution network case study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jer.2026.09.068)  
*Journal of Engineering Research · Available online 7 October 2026 · Corrected proof*

**Main idea.** Combine short-term machine-learning forecasts with linearized power-flow calculations and a safety filter for voltage-constrained distribution-network control.

**Reported results.** The publisher abstract describes a hybrid control framework intended to keep voltage within operating limits. Detailed numerical comparisons are withheld: this reading covered the publisher abstract and article preview, not the complete study.

**Relevance — AI assessment.** For networks with substantial distributed generation, forecast accuracy and feasible control are separate requirements. This architecture is worth examining because it places a physical constraint check between prediction and action. Whether its safety claims extend to nonlinear AC behavior, forecast extremes and changed network topology requires full-text evidence and independent testing.
